# Quick Start: Make Your Website Public in 5 Minutes! 🚀

Choose the fastest option for you:

## 🌟 Option 1: GitHub Pages (EASIEST - 2 minutes)

**What you get:** Free hosting at `https://zliu31.github.io/Cake-3.0/`

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://github.com/zliu31/Cake-3.0/settings/pages
2. Under "Source", select:
   - **Branch:** `claude/check-cake-repo-connection-01C4GYKe1SM3Dou8jpWicnzV`
   - **Folder:** `/ (root)`
3. Click **Save**
4. Wait 1-2 minutes
5. Visit: https://zliu31.github.io/Cake-3.0/

✅ **Done! Your site is live!**

---

## ⚡ Option 2: Netlify (RECOMMENDED - 3 minutes)

**What you get:** Better performance, custom domain, automatic deploys

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://app.netlify.com/start
2. Click **"Import from Git"**
3. Choose **GitHub** and select `zliu31/Cake-3.0`
4. Click **"Deploy site"** (no configuration needed!)
5. Your site is live at: `https://[random-name].netlify.app`

**Bonus:**
- Rename your site: Site settings → Change site name
- Add custom domain: Domain settings → Add custom domain

✅ **Done! Professional hosting activated!**

---

## 🎯 Option 3: Vercel (FAST - 3 minutes)

**What you get:** Lightning-fast CDN, great developer experience

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://vercel.com/new
2. Click **"Import Git Repository"**
3. Select `zliu31/Cake-3.0`
4. Click **"Deploy"**
5. Your site is live at: `https://[project-name].vercel.app`

✅ **Done! Super fast hosting!**
